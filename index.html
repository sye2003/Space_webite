Moon

The moon, Earth's celestial companion, bathes the night sky in its gentle silver glow, a silent witness to the eons of cosmic dance.

Cluster

A cluster is a group of similar objects gathered together in one place. In space, a star cluster is a group of stars held together by gravity. Clusters help scientists study how stars form and evolve.

Colage

A galaxy cluster is a huge collection of galaxies held together by gravity. It may contain hundreds or even thousands of galaxies, along with hot gas and dark matter. Galaxy clusters are among the largest structures in the universe.

Black Hole

A black hole is a region in space where gravity is so strong that nothing, not even light, can escape from it. They are fascinating objects studied by scientists to understand the universe.
